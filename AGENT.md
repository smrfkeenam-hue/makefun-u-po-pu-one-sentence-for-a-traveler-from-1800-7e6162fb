# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb
("One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800").

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`: the modern internet explained to someone from 1800 as
one great library and post office joined, where a letter to any town on
earth is answered before the tea cools. Every word in the sentence was in
use before 1800; no feature list, one picture a traveler could hold.
